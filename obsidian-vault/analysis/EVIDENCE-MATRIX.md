# Evidence Matrix

> Map every major claim against all available evidence. This is the strategic backbone of your case.

---

## How to Read This Matrix

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Claim | The allegation or assertion being evaluated |
| Source | Who made the claim and where |
| Professional Findings | What experts, caseworkers, police found |
| Independent Evidence | Documents, records, third-party observations |
| Contradictions | What conflicts with this claim |
| Status | PROVEN / DISPUTED / UNRESOLVED / DISPROVEN |
| Strength | ★★★ Strong / ★★☆ Moderate / ★☆☆ Weak |

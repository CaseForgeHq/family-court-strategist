- `app/public/scan-questions.js` is the shared versioned question catalogue used by the UI and scan pipeline. The final AI pass receives every question with the extracted context, validated document findings and verified legal records. It must answer each ID once with status, concise answer, finding IDs, legal indexes, limitations and follow-up. Runtime validation rejects missing/duplicate answers and references outside the report. Identical resumed inputs reuse their checkpoint; changed inputs regenerate answers. This adds one model request to new scans.

Older reports remain readable and explicitly show Answer not recorded; they are never retroactively labelled answered. A fresh scan is required to obtain answers.

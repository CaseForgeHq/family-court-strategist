01 / Identity

One mark. Room to breathe.

Use the supplied vector artwork. Keep clear space of at least one quarter of the symbol height. Never stretch the mark or replace the wordmark with typed text.

02 / Colour

Warm surfaces.
Purposeful copper.

Ink for headings, graphite for body text, stone for borders. Reserve brand copper for artwork. Use the darker action copper for small links and white-text buttons.

05 / Layout and language

Keep the work in focus.

8px spacing rhythm, 6px controls and 12px cards. Quiet backgrounds, readable source excerpts and no decorative motion in working screens.

Write with care.

Use “review the source” and “prepare your next steps”. Avoid promises about legal outcomes or claims that AI establishes truth.

Make access clear.

Identify free, preview and planned paid features. Explain local storage and cloud analysis separately.

Keep print useful.

White pages, a small logo, readable references and an explicit draft label. Case preparation documents must remain easy to check.

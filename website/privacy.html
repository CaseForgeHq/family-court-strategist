Desktop app waitlist

Your signup details.

The waitlist collects your name, email address, optional computer type and the time you agreed to receive a desktop app launch update.

What they’re used for

Case Forge uses this list to contact people who asked to hear when the desktop app is ready to try. Joining does not create a paid subscription or request payment details.

What stays out of this form

Do not include case documents or details about your family. The waitlist is separate from the local case workspace. Your local documents are not attached to your signup.

Where details are stored

Signups are stored by the website’s waitlist service. On the public Cloudflare-hosted website, signup details are stored in a private Cloudflare D1 database. When using the local development site, they are stored on the computer running that service. The form confirms success only after the service saves the signup.

Launch messages

No email is sent automatically when you join. Launch messages will include a way to stop further updates. Please submit only your own contact details.

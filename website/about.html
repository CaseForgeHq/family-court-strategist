About Case Forge

Case Forge is an open-source toolkit for parents organising family-court records. It brings documents, timelines, reusable prompts and AI guidance into a folder the user controls.

What you can use today

The free download contains a folder structure, Markdown templates and instructions for an AI assistant with local file access. You can also use the files manually. Created by Kyle Fischer and published by Case Forge. The source is available under the MIT licence.

Inspect the source repository or download the free toolkit. Third-party software and AI services may have their own costs.

Verified facts and source history

The free toolkit now includes a command for recording reviewed facts, preserving their exact wording, reviewing conflicts and checking generated drafts for drift. Fact references update when you generate a new draft; earlier exports remain intact. It requires Node.js 18+ and deliberate human review.

See the complete feature and availability guide, including the distinction between templates, the toolkit command and the local app preview.

What is in development

A local app preview supports document intake and review. A downloadable, paid desktop product with monthly subscriptions is planned. Public installers, pricing and billing are not available yet. The phone images show a responsive preview, not a released iPhone app.

The intended desktop app will let users cancel and keep their files, with active working features requiring a current subscription. Join the desktop waitlist for a launch update.

How we describe the product

Case Forge helps with organisation, source checking and preparation. It is not a law firm, does not provide legal representation and does not promise a court outcome. Public examples use fictional records. AI output needs review against the source.

Questions and corrections
